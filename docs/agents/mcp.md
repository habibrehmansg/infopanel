# AI apps: the MCP tools and the relay

- **AI apps (MCP tools)** (`docs/2.0/mcp.md`): AI apps reach the engine only through the configured service (**AI apps elsewhere**, below), turned on from the paired client. The tools (`Mcp/Tools/`, `McpToolHost`) run in the engine on the API's code paths, each declaring `read` or `edit` (never `session`; `McpToolTests` enumerates them): `list_profiles`, `get_profile` (its items listed within 4 MiB as sent, then "… and N more items"), `list_sensors`, `list_fonts`, `render_preview` (the picture and `Mcp/PreviewChecks.cs` on the same tick of the current revision, `RenderService.Inspect` (the live loop's latest tick when it shows that revision, else a one-off admitted through the process budget as a live tick is, by the loop's runtime when there is one, over it drawn as budget placeholders): text that doesn't fit its box from `TickSnapshot.Texts`, ink past the canvas, ink boxes overlapping unless the container is drawn below, and solid-colour text's contrast with its opacity against the tick drawn without text (`TickSnapshot.Draw(…, withoutText)`); budgets of 4 M sampled pixels and 10,000 pairs; what is skipped listed per item as "Not checked"), and the changes `create_profile` (saved at once), `add_item` (a scene), `add_items` (up to 50 in one commit, a group's children counted: an entry with `children` is a group inserted whole), `update_item` (`group` and `index` move an item, one `move` op, its numbers kept), `update_items` (1 to 50 entries, each `update_item`'s arguments without `profile`, each item once, one commit built by `UpdateItemTool.Ops` per entry in order; an entry's refusal named `items/‹i›`, the engine's 422 paths through a map from op to entry), `remove_item`, `add_asset` (an SVG's text or a picture file's bytes in base64, never a video, checked before it is stored) and `show_on_desktop` (`Mcp/Tools/EditTools.cs`, `ProfileChanges`: one call one commit through `ProfileDocument.Commit`, every op at the revision read and the commit with the document's tag, a conflict read again once, on the profile and items found first; saved, guarded by its own revision, only when the profile was clean just before the commit applied, which `Commit(body, out wasClean)` reads under the commit's lock, otherwise left unsaved and said so); each call is logged with its tool, profile, outcome and time, never its arguments. They are listed by name and input schema only (`McpToolHost.BuiltInList()`, the snapshot `InfoPanel.Tests/Snapshots/mcp-tools.json`): the tools' descriptions, the instructions and the design guide are the configured service's. The preview checks' thresholds are the built-in `Mcp/Text/checks.json` (`McpText`).

- **AI apps elsewhere** (docs/2.0/relay.md; `InfoPanel.Core/Service/RelayClient.cs`, `RelayFrames.cs`, `RelayClock.cs`; `Mcp/RelayHostedService.cs`, `Mcp/Tools/McpCall.cs`): the `service.aiApps` setting (off by default; only `PATCH /api/settings`, so only the session token) keeps one WebSocket open to the configured service's relay (`{address}/svc/v1/relay`) while there is an address and a `present` credential; `GET /api/service`'s `aiApps` is `off`, `connecting`, `connected`, `replaced` (4409: stopped until the setting is turned off and on, or a restart), `outdated` (4426: until an update) or `waiting` (429 or 503 `Retry-After`, 4429 ten minutes). The engine opens it with `ServiceClient`'s rules (`ServiceClient.NewHandler`: no redirect, no proxy; the credential only in the upgrade's `Authorization`); 401 or 4401 rejects the credential by its generation; other failures back off 1 s doubling to 60 s with jitter, cut short by a network change; keepalive pings 20 s, timeout 10 s; turning it off sends `bye`. Frames are text JSON, at most 9 MiB (1009 past it) and 272 levels; the `hello` lists `features` (`design`, `RelayFrames.Features`); only `welcome`, `time`, `call`, `read` and `cancel` are dispatched, anything else is `error` `unsupported`, nothing closes; at most 8 calls and reads (`busy`). **Designs** (`Mcp/Tools/Designs.cs`): a call to `add_item` or `update_item` (`McpToolHost.DesignTools`, with `add_items` and `update_items`, which take `designs`: one per item of its `items` and per child of a group in order, a group's children right after it, `null` for an item given as a scene and for a group, as long as both together, else `malformed`) from elsewhere may bring `design` (`layout`, `source`, `scene`, `origin`: a design the service produced, standing for the arguments' `scene`, `width` and `height`, which beside it are `malformed`) and `files` (texts); `McpCall.Elsewhere` alone carries them, another tool answers `design`; `Designs.Preflight` checks before any write (at most 16 files of 1 MiB as UTF-8, each's SHA-256 one of the design's `source.deps`, every dep a file or stored, no provider's media source), refusing with nothing stored, then the insertion is checked by `ProfileDocument.Check` (the commit's own `Validate`, applying nothing; a conflict rebuilds) and `Designs.Store` stores each file behind the cutoff (the step hook's `write` per file) before the commit, all on the profile found once (`FoundProfile`: its document, and the revision of the name it was found by, checked under the document's lock by every commit attempt). `update_item`'s design carries `basedOn` (`instance`, `rev`, `item`): another tag or item is the error result `basedOn` before anything is stored, never `stale`; its `setDesign` is based on `basedOn.rev` beside the call's other ops at the revision read, one commit; a conflict on that op (the precheck's or the commit's) answers `stale` (`RelayFrames.Stale`) at once, no retry (`ProfileChanges.Apply`'s `isStale`). Without a design, `update_item` may bring the call frame's `basedOn` object, and `update_items` a `basedOn` list (one per entry, `null` or what a read found, never where `designs` has a design; else `malformed`; an object or list on another tool is the error `design`): the same three rules, the op setting the item's design (`width`, `height`, `scene`) based on `basedOn.rev`, a conflict on it `stale`. A `read` frame (`RelayFrame.ReadItem`, admitted as a call: the 8, late, the cutoff's clocks, cancel) reaches `IRelayToolHost.ReadAsync`, answered `item` (`GetProfileTool.ReadItem`: the ids, the tag, the revision and the item whole from one snapshot, with `detail` `get_profile`'s text for it, a sourced item's without scene and source, held to `maxChars` by its structure so it still parses; cancellable until it answers) or an error result (`notFound`, `ambiguous`); `add_items` (`AddItemsTool`, a public tool: 1–50 items, a group's children counted, each checked as `add_item`'s arguments, one commit of an insert per entry (a group with its children inline) and one undo step, all or nothing, the engine's refusals named `items/‹i›/…` and `items/‹i›/children/‹k›/…`; every design preflighted at once); `RelayDesignTests`, `RelayReadTests`, `RelayUpdateDesignTests`, `RelayAddItemsTests`, and `RelayFrameFixtureTests` on `InfoPanel.Core.Tests/Fixtures/relay/frames.json`, one frame of each kind either side sends. Never late: a call whose `sentAt` + 10 s has passed on the relay's clock is refused `late`, read by a `time` challenge counted only within 2 s on both the wall clock and Stopwatch, renewed when over 30 s old on either, void when the two disagree by over 1 s or the wall clock went back (a reading joined in flight is checked again when it comes, and one a call's `sentAt` is over 1 s ahead of is read again, `RelayClock.Contradicts`; the clocks read once as it is checked decide lateness and the deadline, `ClockSnapshot`); a `sentAt` past what a time holds, or text that doesn't decode anywhere in a frame (a member no type reads too), is `malformed`; an outcome of a connection made before the setting was turned off (4409 included) is ignored, bar a refused credential (by its generation), and a 4409 is kept under the lock that finds it current; closing cancels sends waiting or stalled. Tests: `InfoPanel.Tests/Relay/` against a fake relay on a loopback port of its own, both clocks faked (`TwoClocks`).
